Cheesy Ground Beef Potato Casserole

Description

A warm and cozy comfort meal that the whole family will enjoy

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds lean ground beef
  • 1 medium onion chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 10.5 ounce can condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 5 medium russet potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375ºF and grease a 9x13 baking dish
  2. Cook ground beef, onion, and garlic until browned. Drain fat and season with salt, pepper, and paprika
  3. Mix cream of mushroom soup, sour cream, and beef broth in a bowl
  4. Layer sliced potatoes, beef mixture, and soup mixture in the dish. Repeat layers
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes. Remove foil, add cheddar cheese, and bake 15 minutes more
  6. Rest for 10 minutes before serving
