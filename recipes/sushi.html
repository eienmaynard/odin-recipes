Homeade Sushi

Description

Homemade Sushi is a lot easier and cheaper to make then spending a lot on sushi from a resturant. High quality ingredients are used and is a delicious meal to enjoy at the comfort of your home.

Ingredients

  • 6 sheets sushi seaweed
  • 1 batch prepared sushi rice
  • 1/2 lb sashimi-grade raw salmon
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 avocado sliced
  • soy sauce for serving

Steps

  1. Prepare the sushi rice according to the package instructions.
  2. Lay a sheet of seaweed on the bamboo mat.
  3. Spread a thin layer of rice over the seaweed.
  4. Add salmon, cream cheese, and avocado along the center.
  5. Roll tightly using the bamboo mat.
  6. Slice into pieces and serve with soy sauce
