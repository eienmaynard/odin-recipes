Zuppa Toscana

Description

A warm and delicious hearty italian soup that goes well withany season of the year

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 4 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 3 medium potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups kale, chopped
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook bacon in a large pot until crispy. Remove and set aside.
  2. Brown the sausage in the same pot. Drain excess fat.
  3. Sauté onion and garlic until softened.
  4. Add chicken broth and potatoes.Simmer until potatoes are tender.
  5. Stir in kale and heavy cream.Cook for 5 more minutes
  6. Add bacon back in, season with salt and pepper, and serve
